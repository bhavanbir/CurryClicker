# Curry Clicker — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 15, 2026

Curry Clicker ("the game") is developed and published by AB Studios
("we", "us"). Contact: **abstudiosofficialbusiness@gmail.com**

## The short version

The game has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no trackers. We never
sell data. The only personal information we ever receive is what you
voluntarily type into the in-game feedback form.

## Feedback form

If you use the "Contact us" form, we receive:

- your feedback message, and
- your email address, **only if you choose to provide one** (it is optional,
  and used solely to reply to you).

The form is delivered by **FormSubmit.co**, a third-party form relay service,
which forwards your submission to our email inbox. FormSubmit.co processes
the submission only to deliver it; see their terms at
https://formsubmit.co. Submissions remain in our inbox until deleted. To
have your submission or email address deleted, contact us at the address
above.

## Game saves

Your game progress is stored entirely on your own device — in your
browser's local storage (web version) or in a local save file (desktop
version). Save data is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

## Steam version

If you play through Steam, achievements and gameplay stats are reported to
Valve's Steamworks services as part of normal Steam functionality. That
data is handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy:
https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from them. The feedback form should not be
used to submit personal information by anyone under 13.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California,
PIPEDA in Canada), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete
personal information we hold about you. Since the only personal data we
hold is feedback submissions, email us and we will delete them.

## Changes

If our data practices change (for example, if online features are added),
this policy will be updated here with a new effective date.
